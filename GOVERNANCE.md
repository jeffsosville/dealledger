# Governance

## Who runs DealLedger

DealLedger was started by Jeff Sosville ([@jeffsosville](https://github.com/jeffsosville)),
who is currently its only maintainer. As the project grows we will add
maintainers from outside the founder's businesses and write down how
decisions get made.

## Conflicts of interest

Jeff Sosville also owns businesses in the market DealLedger records:

- **ATM Brokerage**, a business brokerage
- **CleaningExits, VendingExits and ATMExits**, marketplaces for selling
  small businesses

Those businesses are recorded in DealLedger like any other broker, by the
same code, on the same schedule. They get no special treatment in coverage,
dates, ranking or presentation. DealLedger does not rank listings at all.

## Commitments

These hold regardless of who funds, owns or maintains the project:

1. **The data stays CC0.** Everything published in `data/` is public domain,
   and data published once is never relicensed or withdrawn.
2. **The data stays ungated.** No login, paywall or lead form stands between
   anyone and the record. Paid services, if any are ever built, sit on top of
   the open data and never replace it.
3. **The method stays public.** Anything that produces a value in the open
   dataset is documented in [docs/METHODOLOGY.md](docs/METHODOLOGY.md) and
   runs from code in this repository.
4. **Corrections are public.** A wrong record is fixed in the open, with its
   history kept.

## Reporting a conflict

If you think any business, including the founder's, is being treated
differently, open an issue. It will be answered in public.
